Walkthrough

Two minutes on the real console, start to finish: a free AI model at work, a hidden attack it falls for, a real Synapse rebuild and the audit log. There is no sound: every step is captioned on screen.

What you will see

  1. A real support ticket. You ask the agent to resolve a ticket in Kestrel Desk, an existing support app.
  2. A free AI model at work. It goes through the MCP tools Synapse generated, on a private copy of the app.
  3. You say yes. The same steps replay on the live app, then live data is checked row by row. Changed your mind? Undo is one click, and it is checked too.
  4. An attack. A ticket hides an instruction and the agent falls for it. Every order it acts on is traced to where it came from, and the reasons are plain rules on screen, not a second AI.
  5. A real Synapse rebuild. The team runs synapse build --discover. Rehearsal reads the new build straight from the generated code, and a proposal rehearsed on the old build cannot be applied.
  6. The audit log. Edit one entry on a copy, and the chain check fails at exactly that entry.
  7. The overview. It counts what was stopped and what went live, from the log.
